# Services Offered

## General Dentistry
- **Routine Checkup & Cleaning** — Comprehensive oral exam, X-rays, professional scaling and polishing.
- **Fillings** — Tooth-coloured composite resin fillings for cavities.
- **Extractions** — Simple and surgical tooth extractions.
- **Root Canal Treatment (RCT)** — Removal of infected pulp to save the natural tooth.
- **Dental Crowns & Bridges** — Porcelain or zirconia crowns; bridges for missing teeth.

## Cosmetic Dentistry
- **Teeth Whitening** — In-office laser whitening and take-home kits.
- **Veneers** — Ultra-thin porcelain or composite shells bonded to the front surface of teeth.
- **Smile Makeover** — Combined cosmetic procedures tailored to the patient's goals.
- **Bonding** — Composite resin applied to repair chips or close gaps.

## Periodontics
- **Scaling & Root Planing** — Deep cleaning below the gumline to treat gum disease and remove plaque/tartar buildup.
- **Gum Surgery** — Surgical treatment for advanced periodontal disease, including flap surgery and pocket reduction.
- **Periodontal Maintenance** — Ongoing follow-up cleanings for patients with a history of gum disease.
- **Gum Grafting** — Tissue grafting to treat gum recession.

## Orthodontics
- **Traditional Braces** — Metal and ceramic braces for alignment correction.
- **Clear Aligners (Invisalign)** — Removable transparent trays for discreet teeth straightening.
- **Retainers** — Post-treatment retention appliances.

## Oral Surgery
- **Wisdom Tooth Removal** — Simple and impacted third-molar extractions.
- **Single Dental Implant** — Titanium post surgically placed to replace one missing tooth.
- **Bone Grafting** — Preparation procedure for implant placement.

## Implantology
- **Full-Arch Implants (All-on-4)** — Fixed full-arch restoration supported by four implants per jaw.
- **Guided Implant Surgery** — Computer-guided placement for complex or multi-implant cases.
- **Multiple Implant Placement** — Cases involving more than one implant site in a single treatment plan.

## Diagnostic Imaging & Radiology
- **Digital X-Rays** — Standard intraoral and panoramic X-rays for cavity detection, diagnosis, and treatment planning.
- **CBCT Scan** — 3D cone-beam imaging used for implant planning, complex extractions, and detailed diagnosis.
- **Diagnostic Consultation** — Review of imaging results with treatment recommendations.

## Paediatric Dentistry
- **Child Checkups** — Gentle exams and cleanings for children aged 2–16.
- **Fluoride Treatment** — Preventive fluoride application.
- **Fissure Sealants** — Protective coatings applied to back teeth.

## Emergency Dentistry
- Toothache relief, chipped or knocked-out tooth, lost filling or crown.
- Same-day emergency slots available Monday–Saturday.
