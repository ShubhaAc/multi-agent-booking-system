# Frequently Asked Questions

## Appointments
**Q: How do I book an appointment?**  
A: You can book through our online chat assistant, call us at +1 (800) 555-2368, or walk in during working hours.

**Q: Can I choose my doctor?**  
A: Yes. You can request a specific doctor when booking, subject to availability.

**Q: How early should I arrive?**  
A: Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment to complete any paperwork.

**Q: What if I need to cancel?**  
A: Cancellations should be made at least 24 hours in advance. Late cancellations may incur a fee.

## Insurance & Payment
**Q: Do you accept insurance?**  
A: Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, and Aetna.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**  
A: We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, and health savings accounts (HSA/FSA).

**Q: Do you offer payment plans?**  
A: Yes, interest-free payment plans are available for treatments over $500. Ask our front desk for details.

## General
**Q: Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?**  
A: Yes, our clinic is fully wheelchair accessible with a dedicated lift and accessible restrooms.

**Q: Do you offer emergency slots?**  
A: Yes. Same-day emergency appointments are available Monday–Saturday. Call us as soon as possible.

**Q: Is parking available?**  
A: Yes, free parking is available in our dedicated lot adjacent to the building. Bike racks are also provided.

**Q: Are children welcome?**  
A: Absolutely. We have a dedicated paediatric team and a child-friendly waiting area.

**Q: What COVID-19 precautions are in place?**  
A: We follow all current health authority guidelines, including enhanced sterilisation, PPE for all staff, and air purification systems in every treatment room.
